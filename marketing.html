From raw delivery exports to reports ready for your business meetings

The app is built around the questions restaurant owners ask every week: How much did we earn? Which platform contributed most? Which store is leading? Are campaigns hurting AOV? What should we focus on next week?

01 · Weekly and Monthly Reports

Summarize performance using real calendar dates

Supports both weekly and monthly reporting. Monthly results are calculated from the first to last day of each month to avoid cross-week confusion.

02 · Platform and Store Comparison

See the full picture, then compare to find gaps

View all-platform summaries or drill into specific platforms and stores to quickly identify differences in revenue, orders, and average order value.

03 · Interactive Charts

Use trend lines, KPI cards, and detail tables together

Charts support hover details, and values can be copied. This is ideal for review meetings, reporting, and store manager coaching.

04 · Complete Export

Export HTML and CSV business reports

Package metrics, definitions, charts, and analytical summaries into one report file for archiving and sharing.

05 · Clear Metric Explanations

Definitions written for operators

Beyond showing numbers, the app explains why each metric matters so owners and teams can make better decisions faster.

06 · Ready for Expansion

Designed to support future integrations

The structure is ready for additional platforms, automated imports, cloud sync, collaboration workflows, and richer business models.

Data is processed locally first, and reports stay under your control

Restaurant operating data is sensitive. Xiangxiaowan Business Assistant is designed to work without account registration and without automatic upload of your files. You control imported and exported data.
