Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Xiangxiaowan Business Assistant handles restaurant delivery data, imported files, exported reports, and local app data.

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Quick summary: Xiangxiaowan Business Assistant helps you organize restaurant delivery data on Mac, generate weekly and monthly reports, and export business analysis files. The app does not require account registration, does not proactively collect personal data, does not sell your data, and does not upload restaurant business data to our servers.

Data We Process

When you use the app, you may import files related to delivery platform or store operations. These files may include revenue, order volume, average order value, platform, store, date, campaign costs, refunds, delivery information, reviews, or other operational metrics.

This data is used to calculate charts, weekly reports, monthly reports, platform comparisons, store comparisons, and exportable reports inside the app.

Where Data Is Stored

  • Imported data is processed primarily on your local device.
  • Exported CSV or HTML reports are saved to locations you choose.
  • If the app stores preferences or temporary files, they are stored in local macOS application storage areas.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your restaurant business data.
  • We do not require registration to analyze local data.
  • We do not proactively upload your imported files or exported reports to our servers.
  • We do not use your operating data for ad-targeting profiles.

Third-Party Services

If future versions introduce cloud sync, account sign-in, payment, crash logging, analytics services, or external platform APIs, we will update this page or show in-app disclosures and request permissions where required.

Exported Reports

The app can export HTML or CSV files that include business metrics, definitions, and charts. After export, these files are managed by you. If you share or upload them to third-party platforms, those platforms' privacy policies apply.

Data Deletion

You can delete imported source files, exported report files, or clear imported data from inside the app. If you remove the app, macOS typically removes app-related local data, but files manually saved to other locations are not automatically deleted.
