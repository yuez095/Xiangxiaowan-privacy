Frequently Asked Questions

Cannot import a file

Confirm the file comes from a supported platform export and that required fields such as headers, dates, amounts, and order counts are complete.

Report numbers look inconsistent

Check selected stores, platforms, date range, and report period. Weekly and monthly views aggregate data differently.

Export failed

Make sure the destination folder is writable and the target file is not already open or locked by another app.

Data privacy

The current app processes imported data locally by default, does not require account registration, and does not proactively upload business reports.

Please include the following in support requests

  • Your app version and macOS version.
  • Steps to reproduce, such as import, filter, chart view, or export actions.
  • Relevant screenshots, error messages, or sample-file notes. Do not send sensitive business data unless you confirm it is safe to share.
  • Your expected outcome, such as successful import, corrected metric behavior, or a specific export format.
